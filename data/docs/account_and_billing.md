# Account & Billing

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. We do not accept wire transfers, cryptocurrency, or checks. Gift cards can be used as a secondary payment method alongside a card, but not as the sole payment method for subscription purchases.

## Subscription billing
Subscriptions renew monthly and are charged on the anniversary date of the original signup. For example, a subscription started on the 15th of the month will always renew on the 15th, or the last day of the month for months that are shorter.

To avoid being charged for the next cycle, the customer must cancel at least 24 hours before the next billing date. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid period — the customer keeps access until then. We do not issue partial refunds for the unused portion of a billing cycle, even if the customer cancels the day after being charged.

Annual subscriptions follow the same 24-hour cancellation rule but renew once per year instead of monthly, and carry a 20% discount compared to paying monthly.

## Updating payment details
Customers can update their card on file from Account Settings > Payment Methods. Updated cards apply to the next scheduled charge; they do not retroactively charge for a past failed payment. If a renewal charge fails, we retry automatically on days 1, 3, and 7 after the failed attempt before the subscription is paused.

## Password and account recovery
If a customer is locked out, send a password reset link to the email on file. Reset links expire after 30 minutes. If the customer no longer has access to that email address, they must verify their identity through a support ticket with their order number and billing zip code before the email on file can be changed.

## Closing an account
Closing an account is permanent and cannot be undone. It cancels any active subscription immediately (following the same no-partial-refund rule) and deletes saved payment methods, but order history is retained for 7 years for tax and compliance purposes.

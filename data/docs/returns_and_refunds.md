# Returns & Refunds Policy

## Return window
Unopened items in original packaging may be returned for a full refund within 30 days of delivery. Opened items may be returned within 14 days of delivery, but only if the item is defective or damaged. A 15% restocking fee applies to opened, non-defective returns. Seasonal and holiday items follow the same 30-day window unless the product page states otherwise.

To start a return, the customer opens a return request from Order History and selects a reason code. The system generates a prepaid return label for defective items; for non-defective returns the customer pays return shipping unless they hold a Premium membership, in which case return shipping is always free.

## Damaged or defective products
Report a damaged or defective product within 7 days of delivery by opening a support ticket with photos of the damage. Do not ask the customer to return a damaged item before a replacement ships — the replacement should go out first, and the damaged unit can be picked up by the carrier at the same address.

Once approved, we ship a free replacement immediately, or issue a full refund if the customer prefers not to receive a replacement. If the item is out of stock, the agent must offer a full refund rather than a delayed replacement.

## How refunds are processed
Refunds are issued within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item at our warehouse. The 5-7 day window starts from warehouse receipt, not from the day the customer ships the item — factor in transit time when setting expectations with the customer.

Refunds are always issued to the original payment method used for the purchase. We do not issue refunds as cash, check, gift card, or cryptocurrency under any circumstances, even if the customer requests it. If the original payment method has expired (for example an old card), the refund still routes to the issuing bank, which typically applies it to the replacement card automatically; this can take an extra 3-5 business days.

## Non-returnable items
Gift cards, digital downloads, and clearance items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned or refunded. Personalized or custom-engraved items are also final sale unless they arrived damaged or defective.

## Frequently asked agent questions
- If a customer returns an item after the 30-day window because of a documented shipping delay on our end, agents may make a one-time courtesy exception and approve the return.
- A restocking fee is never applied to a defective or damaged item, only to non-defective opened returns.
- Store credit is not offered as an alternative to a refund; only the original payment method is used.

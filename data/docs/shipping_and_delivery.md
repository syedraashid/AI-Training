# Shipping & Delivery

## Shipping options
Standard shipping is handled by RegionalGo Post and takes 3-5 business days. Standard shipping is free on orders over $35; orders under $35 pay a flat $4.99 fee. Express shipping is handled by SwiftCourier and takes 1-2 business days for an additional fee of $12.99. There is also an overnight option on SwiftCourier for $24.99, available only for orders placed before 1 PM local time.

We currently ship only within the domestic country. We do not offer international shipping at this time, including to territories and APO/FPO military addresses. Customers asking about international shipping should be told this is on the roadmap but has no committed date.

## Tracking
A tracking link is emailed automatically once the order ships. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear after the label is created, so a customer seeing "label created" with no movement on day one is normal and not a lost package.

## Lost or missing packages
If tracking shows a package as "delivered" but the customer did not receive it, the customer must file a lost-package claim within 48 hours of the delivery scan. Claims filed after 48 hours can still be reviewed case-by-case but are not guaranteed.

Once a claim is filed, we open an investigation with the carrier. If the package is not located within 5 business days, we reship the order at no cost or issue a full refund, whichever the customer prefers. Agents should never promise a reship on the first contact — the investigation step is required first, except for orders under $20, which can be reshipped immediately without waiting for the carrier investigation.

## Address changes
The shipping address can be changed only before the order status shows "processing." Once an order is processing, the address cannot be changed and the order must be cancelled and reordered instead. If the order has already shipped to the wrong address because the customer entered it incorrectly, the customer is responsible for reshipping costs; if the error was on our system's side, we cover the reshipping cost.

## Delivery instructions and signature requirements
Orders over $200 require an adult signature on delivery. Customers may add delivery instructions (such as "leave with doorman") at checkout, but SwiftCourier overnight shipments always require a signature regardless of order value.

# Warranty & Repairs

## Standard warranty
Every product includes a standard warranty covering manufacturing defects for 12 months from the original purchase date. Proof of purchase is required for any warranty claim; a bank or credit card statement showing the purchase is accepted if the original receipt is lost.

## What the warranty excludes
The standard warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage, cosmetic wear such as scratches from normal use, or damage from unauthorized repairs or modifications. Opening the product casing yourself voids the warranty immediately, even if the original defect is unrelated to the area that was opened.

## Extended warranty
Customers may purchase an extended warranty within 30 days of the original purchase date. The extended warranty adds an additional 12 months of coverage on top of the standard warranty, for a total of 24 months of combined coverage. The extended warranty covers the same manufacturing defects as the standard warranty and does not add any additional coverage types — it only extends the time window.

The extended warranty cannot be purchased after the 30-day window has passed, and it cannot be purchased at all for clearance or "Final Sale" items.

## Filing a warranty claim
Warranty claims are filed the same way as damaged-product reports: open a support ticket with proof of purchase and a description of the defect, including photos or video if the defect is intermittent. Approved claims are repaired or replaced at no cost to the customer, including return shipping for the defective unit.

## Repair turnaround
Once a defective unit is received at our repair center, standard repairs take 10-14 business days. If a repair is not possible, the unit is replaced with a new or refurbished equivalent model instead, at our discretion.
